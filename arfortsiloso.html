Sentosa x Enterprise: AR Fort Siloso

2021 | Promotional Video | Internship

Synopsis

Featuring an interactive storyline, the different historic exhibits at Fort Siloso were brought to life through the AR mobile application. The immersive experience enabled guests to have a deeper perspective of the rich heritage of Fort Siloso.

Process Work

Storyboard

During the location scout during our planning phase, we had to the opportunity to take pictures for the storyboard to guide the flow of the video. Meant as a guide, the video that was produced does not 100% look like the storyboard. We had to make changes due to logistical or technical reasons, or just had better ideas on the day of shoot.

Camera Plan

Camera plans help me communicate with logistical department of the crew to plan shots according to area to minimize movement and filming time. It goes hand in hand with the storyboard to help guide my angles and talent positioning during the shoot.
