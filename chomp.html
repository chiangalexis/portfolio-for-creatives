Chomp

Our team designed a Cooking tutorial and Meal Planner App titled: Chomp. The app that aims to equip recent graduates starting to live independently with the culinary knowledge and build their confidence in the kitchen. We decided to focus on this topic as we wanted to provide aid in the problems recently independent people face when living on the own for the first time, cooking was one of them.
