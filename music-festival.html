Seeing, Hearing — Music Festival

Wash away your worries with the heartfelt performances of Chinese Indie Artists as they pour their soul into the messages of their songs. Hearing, Seeing is a fictional music festival for those that are seeking for a safe haven through music.

Concept & Rationale

Hearing, Seeing is a fictional music festival that features slow, romantic, and soft music. The poster aims to reflect the solemn emotion.

Calligraphy is personally done by hand, and animated. It aims to reflect the personal touch and letter writing.

Collaterals

Since this was a nostalgia themed music festival, it felt right to have physical tickets. Social media promotion is simple with the artists' name written and animated, visually consistent with the poster.

Augmented Reality

To make the festival experience more interactive, augmented reality was a tool to deliver a more immersive experience. By using Adobe Aero, I was able to make the ticket interactive if the user were to scan it with their phone.
