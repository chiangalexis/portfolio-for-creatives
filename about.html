  • Hi, I'm Alexis!

    Born and raised in the sunny island of Singapore, I embrace the kampung spirit; the act of fostering a strong sense of community and sharing. I believe that transfers into my role as a team player and purposeful intentions when making design choices.

    Stories and the human connection move me and inspire me to create and collaborate with people. My quest to learn about the different forms of communication did not stop at my diploma in filmmaking; it persevered through to my bachelor’s in visual communications.

    You can find me either behind the lens, in front of the laptop, or on my side quest to find the best sambal belachan.
