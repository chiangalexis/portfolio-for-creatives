Remedy

Remedy is a health app that aims to bring a sense of calm within the storm. A daily planner, a glossary that provides knowledge, and a place to log all medical history. With its vibrant palette, it aims to bring a sense of funk and quirk into everyday lives.

Team

Alexis Chiang Web Design

Scarlett Side Branding & Social Media

Tools

Figma

Adobe Illustrator

Adobe Photoshop

Problem Statement

Adults with chronic illness need an easy, accessible way to access medical information and plan their day according to their energy level, because they would like to relieve their medical stress and spend time with loved ones.

Rationale

Neatly tucked away in your pocket, Remedy’s clean shapes and large buttons aims to declutter your day and helps you focus on one thing at a time. You can go about your day at your pace, at your capacity, and at your preference.
