Remedy

Remedy is a health app that aims to bring a sense of calm within the storm. A daily planner, a glossary that provides knowledge, and a place to log all medical history. With its vibrant palette, it aims to bring a sense of funk and quirk into everyday lives.

One of our key feature is an assistive chatbot that is integrated, ready to use at any page, just call for help using the hotline button.

Step by Step Guide

Make your meal

The app provides a step by step tutorial on making the dish, lists the ingredients you need, and teaches kitchen skills, in this case mincing, within this flow.

Users can access the tab above for an overview of the steps or ingredients needed, as well as Chompley.

They can also swipe their screen or hover their hand and wave left or right to access the next step or go back one step. This is done in consideration of potential scenarios where users do not want to touch their screens due to mess in the kitchen.

Dishes

Cater to your preferences

So it turns out, the user can actually look at the individual dishes, and their unique stats and perp times and stuff like that. One of our main design solution ideas for the target audience was keeping a lot of flexibility in terms of ingredients, so anyone no matter their preference can enjoy the meal.

The Dishes page is where you can view different dishes made from Chomp, or made from your own recipe. Here you can see the ingredients, ratings, stats of the dish (difficulty, preferences, tags) and also the time it takes to make and prepare it.
